There is no universal winner. There is fit for the product you are shipping now—and the team that will own it.

## Choose Django when

You need batteries-included admin, ORM maturity, permissions, and rapid enterprise module delivery (ERP, CRM, internal tools).

## Choose FastAPI when

You need high-performance APIs, async I/O, OpenAPI-first contracts, and AI/data services with clear service boundaries.

## Hybrid is valid

Many successful platforms keep domain systems in Django and expose specialized AI or realtime services via FastAPI. Consistency of auth and observability matters more than purity.
