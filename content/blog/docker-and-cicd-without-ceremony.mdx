Shipping should be routine. If releases feel like events, the pipeline is incomplete.

## One image, many environments

Build once, configure with environment variables, and keep secrets out of the image. Nginx and process managers exist to make that predictable.

## Tests that gate merges

Lint, typecheck, and critical path tests on every PR. Skip vanity coverage theater; protect the flows that move money and data.

## Deploy with rollback

Blue/green or simple previous-tag rollbacks beat heroic debugging at midnight. Observability after deploy is part of the release, not an afterthought.
