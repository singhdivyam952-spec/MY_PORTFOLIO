Enterprise teams do not need another chatbot demo. They need answers grounded in their documents—with citations, latency budgets, and operational reliability.

## Start with the business question

Before choosing a vector database, define who asks what, how wrong answers are handled, and what “good enough” means for trust. RAG quality is a product problem first.

## Ingestion is half the system

Chunking strategy, metadata, and refresh cadence determine retrieval quality more than prompt cleverness. Prefer semantic chunks with stable IDs and clear source attribution.

## Retrieval then generation

Hybrid search (keyword + vector), reranking, and source filters reduce hallucinations. Stream responses for UX, but never skip citation surfaces for high-stakes domains.

## Evaluate continuously

Offline golden sets and online feedback loops catch regressions when models or corpora change. Production AI without evaluation is guesswork.
